# Cryptolandia

Cryptolandia was a crypto tracker for iPhone and iPad, built around calm and clarity rather than noise: live prices, interactive charts and curated news, with no data collection.

## The problem

Most crypto trackers were cluttered and loud — dense interfaces, aggressive notifications and questionable data practices.

## The approach

A tracker that prioritised readability. Live prices with clear colour-coded changes, smooth charts with intuitive time ranges, a compact multi-source news reader, eleven display currencies, several list styles, and optional Face ID or Touch ID lock. Privacy wasn’t a feature; it was the architecture.

## The result

A complete native iOS app — designed, built and shipped to the App Store end to end. The project sharpened the studio’s process for taking a product from concept to launch.
